## Top 5 Critical Issues

1. **DOE IDIQ Ceiling Exhaustion (RF-11):** $45M ceiling 72.7% consumed; projected exhaustion April–May 2025 at current run rate. FY2025 DOE revenue could fall from $24.4M to ≤$12.3M — a $12.1M headwind not reflected in the $182–$205M valuation.

2. **EBITDA Discrepancy & Aggressive Add-Backs (RF-01, RF-02):** CIM shows $22.8M; QoE shows $23.8M — $1.0M gap unreconciled. The $1.2M "litigation settlement" add-back is actually a cash *receipt*, not an expense. Rebased defensible Adj. EBITDA: ~$19.5M–$21.0M, implying EV of $156M–$189M at 8–9x.

3. **Salt Lake City Lease / Permit Dual Default (RF-16, RF-09):** Landlord consent to 2022 Pinnacle assignment never received (32 months pending); landlord sent compliance inquiry November 2024; proposed transaction triggers a second CoC event. Facility serves ~$16.2M revenue; Utah permit (UTH-0441) still in Pinnacle's name.

4. **Grand Junction Environmental Liability (RF-06):** 2019 Phase II ESA (5+ years stale) identified petroleum hydrocarbons and chlorinated solvents (DNAPL). Vapor intrusion risk to occupied building unassessed. 2019 cost estimate of $1.8M–$2.6M excludes vapor intrusion, off-site remediation, and third-party claims. **No insurance coverage** (PLL gradual contamination exclusion applies).

5. **Change-of-Control Cascade (RF-11, RF-12, RF-16, RF-21):** The 100% stock acquisition simultaneously triggers: mandatory bank debt prepayment ($38.96M), CoC provisions in 8 customer contracts ($53M revenue), assignment/consent requirements in 6+ leases, $2.625M single-trigger executive severance, and DOE novation requirements.
