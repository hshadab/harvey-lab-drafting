3. **Salt Lake City Lease — Dual Consent Failure (RF-18)** — Landlord consent requested April 2022; no response in 32 months. Sycamore acquisition = second CoC event. Landlord sent inquiry letter November 2024. Facility serves $16.2M revenue and operates under permit still in Pinnacle's name.
