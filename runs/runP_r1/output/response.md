## Top 10 Most Critical Findings

1. **$1.0M EBITDA Discrepancy** — CIM says $22.8M; QoE data package says $23.8M; management cannot identify the reconciling item. At 8–9x, this is an $8M–$9M valuation swing.

2. **Reported EBITDA Declined 27% YoY** — From $25.2M (FY2023) to $18.4M (FY2024) while revenue grew 11%. All $5.4M in add-backs are concentrated in one year; integration costs appear in FY2022, FY2023, and FY2024 (three consecutive years — not non-recurring).

3. **DOE IDIQ Ceiling Near Exhaustion** — $32.7M of $45.0M ceiling consumed (72.7%) in 61.7% of the contract period. At current run rate, ceiling exhausted by April–May 2025 — the target closing date. Potential **$12.1M+ FY2025 revenue headwind** (9.5% of total revenue) not disclosed in the CIM.

4. **Salt Lake City Lease — Dual Consent Failure** — Landlord consent for the Pinnacle→RES assignment was requested April 2022 and never received. The Sycamore acquisition is a *second* change-of-control event. The SLC facility serves ~$16.2M of revenue and houses the SLC hazardous waste permit. Landlord sent a compliance inquiry in November 2024.

5. **Grand Junction Phase II ESA Stale (5+ Years)** — 2019 estimate of $1.8M–$2.6M is almost certainly understated. Vapor intrusion risk to occupied maintenance building unassessed. Off-site plume containment unconfirmed. Gradual contamination explicitly excluded from pollution liability insurance — **entirely uninsured**.

6. **Fleet Replacement Backlog ~$9.8M** — 44.7% of fleet past useful life. True normalized maintenance CapEx is ~$7.3M–$7.8M/year vs. management's $4.0M–$4.5M. Implies **$26M–$30M downward EV adjustment** at 8–9x.

7. **NLRB Unionization Petition** — Teamsters Local 455 filed November 4, 2024 covering 62 field technicians at Grand Junction and Casper. Disclosed only in a **footnote** to the benefits summary — not in the CIM or LOI.

8. **$2.625M Single-Trigger CoC Severance** — CEO and COO each receive $1.3125M payable within 30 days of closing regardless of post-closing employment. Classification as Transaction Expense vs. buyer-borne cost is unresolved in the LOI.

9. **Litigation Settlement Add-Back Misidentified** — The $1.2M "litigation settlement" add-back is actually a cash *receipt* from the Clearwater Logistics settlement — not a cost. Adding back a cash receipt inflates EBITDA and is methodologically incorrect.

10. **FY2024 Financials Reviewed, Not Audited** — Audited statements (FY2022–FY2024) are a hard closing condition per LOI §8(i). Current reviewer is a small Grand Junction CPA firm. Audit may surface restatements.
