## KEY FINDINGS SUMMARY

### CRITICAL ISSUES (Must Resolve Pre-Closing)

**1. DOE Contract Revenue Cliff**
- 19.2% of revenue ($24.4M) from DOE IDIQ contract
- Only $12.3M remaining ceiling capacity
- Ceiling will be exhausted by April-May 2025 (6 months from target closing)
- Potential $12.1M+ revenue decline in FY2025 (9.5% of total revenue)
- **Action**: Immediate DOE Contracting Officer engagement

**2. Mandatory Debt Prepayment**
- $38.7M outstanding debt with change-of-control prepayment provision
- Requires ~$39.2M cash at closing plus 1.0% premium ($263K)
- No waiver mechanism available
- **Action**: Negotiate refinancing or payoff with High Plains National Bank

**3. Environmental Liability**
- Grand Junction facility ($6.8M appraised value) has documented contamination
- Petroleum hydrocarbons and chlorinated solvents (TCE, PCE)
- Phase II ESA (June 2019) estimated $1.8M-$2.6M remediation cost
- No updated assessment in 5.5 years; vapor intrusion risk identified
- **Action**: Commission updated Phase II ESA and vapor intrusion assessment

### HIGH-SEVERITY ISSUES (Require Contractual Protections)

**4. Regulatory Enforcement**
- CDPHE Notice of Violation (July 2024) for improper waste storage
- Penalty exposure: $200K-$1.16M depending on violation period
- Settlement negotiations ongoing

**5. Lease Assignment Risk**
- Salt Lake City facility lease requires landlord consent for change of control
- Assignment request from Pinnacle acquisition (April 2022) unapproved
- Facility generates $16.2M annual revenue (12.7% of total)
- Proposed acquisition may trigger additional consent requirement

**6. Executive Severance**
- Total severance obligations: $3.775M
- Single-trigger (Calloway brothers): $2.625M payable at closing
- Double-trigger (other executives): $1.15M contingent on termination
- Represents 16.5% of enterprise value at midpoint

## TRANSACTION RECOMMENDATION

**PROCEED WITH ACQUISITION** subject to:

1. **Critical Issue Resolution**
   - DOE ceiling modification or new contract confirmation
   - Debt refinancing/payoff arrangement
   - Updated environmental assessment
   - CDPHE enforcement settlement

2. **Purchase Price Adjustments**
   - Environmental remediation holdback ($2M-$3M)
   - Regulatory/litigation contingency holdback ($1M-$2M)
   - DOE revenue performance earn-out
   - Severance reduction negotiation

3. **Contractual Protections**
   - Extended survival periods for environmental/regulatory reps (6 years)
   - Specific representations on DOE contract, environmental conditions, litigation
   - Landlord consent conditions for lease assignments
   - Permit transfer completion conditions

4. **Closing Conditions**
   - Landlord consent to Salt Lake City lease assignment
   - Permit transfer completion (UTH-0441)
   - DOE Contracting Officer approval (if required)
   - Updated environmental assessment
   - CDPHE enforcement resolution
