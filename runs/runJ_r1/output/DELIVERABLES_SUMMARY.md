## Key Findings

### Revenue & Growth Concerns
- **DOE Concentration**: 19.2% of revenue ($24.4M) from single customer with IDIQ ceiling exhaustion projected April-May 2025
- **Customer Concentration**: Top 10 customers = 67% of revenue; top 5 = 50.4%
- **Growth Deceleration**: FY2024 revenue growth of 11.1% is lowest in 4-year period (CAGR 16.8%)
- **EBITDA Decline**: Reported EBITDA declined 27% YoY ($25.2M to $18.4M)

### Financial & Structural Issues
- **Debt Refinancing**: $38.7M outstanding with mandatory prepayment on change of control; $263K prepayment premium if closing Jan-Jan 2026
- **Severance Obligations**: $3.775M total change-of-control severance ($2.625M single-trigger for founders)
- **EBITDA Adjustments**: $5.4M in adjustments excluded from bank definition creates $35M-$58M valuation gap
- **Capex Backlog**: 44.7% of fleet at or past useful life; $9.8M replacement cost; $3.3M/year catch-up capex needed

### Environmental & Regulatory Risks
- **Grand Junction Contamination**: $1.8M-$2.6M remediation cost; Phase II ESA from June 2019 (no update); vapor intrusion risk
- **CDPHE Enforcement**: Notice of Violation for improper waste storage; $200K-$1.16M penalty exposure; settlement pending
- **Permit Status**: Salt Lake City permit still in Pinnacle name; transfer application pending since March 2022
- **Asbestos Liability**: 11-year service line (FY2012-FY2023); 2 open workers' comp claims; pre-2015 work not covered by current policy

### Operational & Legal Risks
- **Lease Assignment**: Salt Lake City facility lease requires landlord consent; assignment requested April 2022 but NOT YET CONSENTED; $16.2M revenue at risk
- **Litigation**: Ramirez personal injury action ($4.8M damages sought); coverage determination pending; $500K-$1.5M uninsured exposure
- **Wage Claim**: Threatened FLSA/COMPS wage claim from 8 former asbestos technicians; $100K-$200K exposure
- **Union Organizing**: NLRB election petition filed November 2024 for 62 field technicians (12.8% of workforce)

## Recommended Immediate Actions

### Pre-SPA Signing (Critical)
1. Obtain written landlord consent to Salt Lake City lease assignment
2. Negotiate debt refinancing or payoff commitment with High Plains National Bank
3. Obtain CDPHE settlement agreement or penalty determination
4. Obtain insurance coverage determination on Ramirez litigation
5. Commission updated Phase II ESA at Grand Junction facility
6. Conduct vapor intrusion assessment of occupied building

### Pre-Closing Due Diligence
1. Detailed customer concentration and retention analysis
2. Environmental remediation cost estimate update
3. Litigation risk assessment and tail coverage evaluation
4. Fleet condition assessment and replacement plan
5. Permit transfer status confirmation
6. Management retention agreement negotiations

### SPA Negotiation Points
1. EBITDA definition and adjustment categories
2. Environmental representations and survival periods
3. Litigation and regulatory matter representations
4. Customer concentration and revenue decline representations
5. Change-of-control severance treatment
6. Lease assignment and permit transfer conditions to closing
