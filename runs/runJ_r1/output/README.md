# Ridgeline Environmental Services, Inc. - Acquisition Due Diligence Red Flag Analysis

## Project Overview

This analysis reviews the proposed acquisition of Ridgeline Environmental Services, Inc. ("RES") by Sycamore Capital Partners, LP ("Project Ridgeline"). The analysis identifies and categorizes 20 material red flags discovered during comprehensive review of the Company's data room documents.

## Key Findings

### Revenue & Growth
- **DOE Concentration**: 19.2% of revenue with IDIQ ceiling exhaustion projected April-May 2025
- **Customer Concentration**: Top 10 customers = 67% of revenue
- **Growth Deceleration**: FY2024 growth of 11.1% is lowest in 4-year period
- **EBITDA Decline**: Reported EBITDA declined 27% YoY

### Financial Structure
- **Debt**: $38.7M outstanding with mandatory prepayment on change of control
- **Severance**: $3.775M total change-of-control obligations
- **EBITDA Gap**: $5.4M in adjustments excluded from bank definition
- **Capex**: $9.8M fleet replacement backlog

### Environmental & Regulatory
- **Contamination**: $1.8M-$2.6M remediation cost at Grand Junction facility
- **Enforcement**: CDPHE Notice of Violation with $200K-$1.16M penalty exposure
- **Permits**: Salt Lake City permit transfer pending since March 2022
- **Asbestos**: 11-year service line with 2 open workers' comp claims

### Operational & Legal
- **Lease**: Salt Lake City facility consent pending; $16.2M revenue at risk
- **Litigation**: Ramirez personal injury action with $500K-$1.5M uninsured exposure
- **Wage Claim**: Threatened FLSA claim with $100K-$200K exposure
- **Union**: NLRB election petition for 62 field technicians

## Recommended Immediate Actions

### Pre-SPA Signing (Critical)
1. Obtain written landlord consent to Salt Lake City lease assignment
2. Negotiate debt refinancing or payoff commitment
3. Obtain CDPHE settlement agreement or penalty determination
4. Obtain insurance coverage determination on Ramirez litigation
5. Commission updated Phase II ESA at Grand Junction facility
6. Conduct vapor intrusion assessment

### Pre-Closing Due Diligence

1. EBITDA definition and adjustment categories
2. Environmental representations and survival periods
3. Litigation and regulatory matter representations
4. Customer concentration and revenue decline representations
5. Change-of-control severance treatment
6. Lease assignment and permit transfer conditions

## Risk Assessment Framework

Red flags are categorized by severity level:

- **CRITICAL**: Issues requiring immediate resolution or creating material transaction risk; must be addressed pre-signing
- **HIGH**: Significant issues requiring detailed due diligence and mitigation; material financial or operational impact
- **MEDIUM**: Moderate issues requiring attention and contingency planning; manageable with appropriate mitigation
- **LOW**: Administrative or lower-priority issues; minimal transaction impact

## Conclusion

The proposed acquisition of Ridgeline Environmental Services, Inc. presents a compelling platform opportunity in the fragmented Mountain West environmental services market. However, the transaction is subject to material risks that require careful due diligence and mitigation planning.

The four CRITICAL issues must be resolved or substantially mitigated prior to signing the definitive purchase agreement. The eight HIGH-severity issues require detailed due diligence to quantify financial exposure and develop appropriate representations, warranties, and indemnification provisions.

With appropriate due diligence and risk mitigation, the transaction can proceed on a sound financial and operational basis.
