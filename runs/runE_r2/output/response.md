## Top 5 Issues Requiring Immediate Action

1. **DOE IDIQ ceiling exhaustion** — At the current $2.03M/month run rate, the remaining $12.3M ceiling is exhausted by April–May 2025 (coinciding with target closing). DOE revenue could fall 50%+ in FY2025. Not disclosed in the CIM.

2. **$1.0M unexplained EBITDA discrepancy** — The CIM shows $22.8M Adjusted EBITDA; the QoE workbook shows $23.8M. Management has acknowledged the gap but cannot identify the source. The entire $182–205M EV is anchored to this figure.

3. **Grand Junction Phase II ESA is 5+ years stale** — The 2019 assessment (which the ESA itself says should be updated before any transaction) has not been refreshed. Vapor intrusion is unassessed; off-site plume migration is unconfirmed; DNAPL risk is unquantified. The PLL policy explicitly excludes this contamination.

4. **Salt Lake City lease — dual assignment default** — The Pinnacle-to-RES assignment has been unresolved for 2.5 years. The landlord sent an inquiry in November 2024. The proposed Sycamore acquisition would be a second change-of-control event. This facility serves ~$16.2M (12.7%) of revenue and houses the unresolved Utah permit.

5. **NLRB Teamsters petition** — Filed November 4, 2024 covering 62 field technicians at the two largest facilities. Disclosed only in a footnote. The CIM's representation that the workforce is "entirely non-unionized" is materially incomplete.
