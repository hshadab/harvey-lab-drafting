## Key Findings

### Critical Issues (3)
1. **DOE Contract Revenue Cliff** - $12.1M+ potential revenue decline if ceiling not increased
2. **Mandatory Debt Prepayment** - $39.2M+ cash requirement at closing
3. **Environmental Liability** - $1.8M-$2.6M remediation cost at owned facility

### High Severity Issues (6)
- Customer concentration (top 10 = 67% of revenue)
- CDPHE regulatory enforcement ($200K-$1.16M penalty)
- Pending litigation ($500K-$1.5M exposure)
- Lease assignment risk ($16.2M revenue at risk)
- Permit transfer delays (2.75 years incomplete)
- Executive severance obligations ($3.775M)

### Medium Severity Issues (6)
- EBITDA adjustment discrepancies
- Fleet replacement backlog ($9.8M)
- Asbestos tail liability (uninsured)
- Threatened labor action (NLRB petition)
- Insurance coverage gaps
- Management continuity risk

### Low Severity Issues (3)
- Threatened wage claim
- Pinnacle integration incomplete
- Lease expiration timing
