## KEY FINDINGS

### Critical Issues Requiring Immediate Resolution

1. **DOE Contract Ceiling Exhaustion**
   - 19.2% of revenue ($24.4M) from DOE IDIQ contract
   - $45M ceiling 72.7% consumed; only $12.3M remaining
   - At current run rate, ceiling exhausted by April-May 2025
   - Potential FY2025 revenue decline: $12.1M (9.5% of total revenue)
   - Enterprise value impact: $20M-$27M reduction at 8-9x multiple

2. **Debt Change-of-Control Prepayment**
   - $38.7M outstanding debt with mandatory prepayment on change of control
   - Total prepayment obligation: $39.2M-$39.3M (including premium and accrued interest)
   - No consent or waiver mechanism available
   - Must be refinanced or paid off at closing

3. **Salt Lake City Lease Assignment**
   - Lease contains change-of-control clause requiring landlord consent
   - Assignment from Pinnacle to RES requested April 12, 2022 but NOT CONSENTED TO
   - Facility generates $16.2M annual revenue (12.7% of total)
   - Landlord could withhold consent to Sycamore acquisition or demand rent increase

4. **Change-of-Control Severance**
   - Total severance obligation: $3.775M
   - Single-trigger severance to Calloway brothers: $2.625M (payable at closing)
   - Double-trigger severance to other executives: $1.15M (if terminated within 12 months)
   - Reduces purchase price or increases buyer's post-closing costs

### High-Risk Issues Requiring Significant Attention

- **Customer Concentration:** 67% of revenue from top 10 customers; 30.6% from top 2
- **Environmental Liability:** Grand Junction facility contamination; estimated remediation cost $1.8M-$2.6M
- **Regulatory Enforcement:** CDPHE penalty exposure $200K-$1.16M for hazardous waste storage violation
- **Litigation Risk:** Ramirez personal injury action; potential uninsured liability $500K-$1.5M
- **EBITDA Adjustment Discrepancy:** $35M-$58M valuation gap between management and bank EBITDA definitions
- **Founder Dependency:** Key person risk if founders depart post-closing

### Medium-Risk Issues Requiring Management

- Fleet age and deferred capex ($9.8M replacement backlog)
- Asbestos tail liability (unquantifiable long-tail exposure)
- Threatened labor action (NLRB unionization petition)
- Threatened wage claim ($100K-$200K exposure)
- Revenue decline and margin compression (growth deceleration)
- Insurance coverage gaps (asbestos and environmental exclusions)

## CONCLUSION

While Ridgeline Environmental Services represents an attractive platform with strong market positioning, recurring revenue characteristics, and experienced management, the identified red flags present material risks requiring careful attention and mitigation before closing.

The four critical issues – DOE contract ceiling exhaustion, debt change-of-control prepayment, Salt Lake City lease assignment, and change-of-control severance – must be resolved or adequately mitigated in the transaction structure.

The aggregate financial exposure from identified contingencies and liabilities is substantial ($67.5M-$70.5M) and should be carefully factored into the purchase price and transaction structure. Buyer should consider earn-out provisions, price adjustments, and representations & warranties insurance to manage identified risks.
