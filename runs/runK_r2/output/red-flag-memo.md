## CRITICAL RISKS DETAIL

### RF-001: DOE Contract Ceiling Exhaustion

**Issue:** The Company's largest customer relationship is the U.S. Department of Energy (DOE), representing 19.2% of trailing twelve-month revenue ($24.4M). This revenue is generated under IDIQ Contract No. DE-EM-0005523, a five-year contract with a $45M ceiling value. As of September 30, 2024, the Company has utilized $32.7M of the ceiling (72.7%), leaving only $12.3M remaining.

**Risk:** At the current run rate of approximately $24.4M annually ($2.03M monthly), the remaining $12.3M ceiling will be exhausted by approximately April-May 2025. The contract does not expire until August 31, 2026, leaving approximately 16 months of unfunded contract period. If the ceiling is not increased or a new contract awarded, DOE revenue could decline from $24.4M (FY2024) to approximately $12.3M or less in FY2025, representing a potential revenue headwind of $12.1M (9.5% of current total revenue).

**Financial Impact:** $12.1M+ revenue decline = $96.8M-$108.9M reduction in enterprise value at 8-9x EBITDA multiple. Represents 5-6% of proposed transaction value.

**Mitigation:** (1) Immediate engagement with DOE Contracting Officer to discuss ceiling modification or new contract opportunities; (2) Develop contingency revenue plan; (3) Obtain representation from sellers regarding DOE contract status and renewal prospects; (4) Consider earnout provisions tied to DOE revenue maintenance.

### RF-003: Debt Change-of-Control Prepayment Obligation

**Issue:** The Company maintains a senior secured credit facility with High Plains National Bank totaling $38.7M ($12.4M revolving facility + $26.3M term loan). The credit agreement contains a mandatory prepayment provision triggered by a change of control, defined as acquisition of 50%+ of outstanding equity.

**Risk:** The proposed 100% stock acquisition by Sycamore Capital would trigger the change-of-control provision, requiring full prepayment of all outstanding obligations within five (5) business days of closing. If closing occurs between January 17, 2025 and January 16, 2026, a prepayment premium of 1.0% applies to the term loan ($263K). Total estimated prepayment obligation: $39.2M-$39.3M.

**Financial Impact:** $39.2M+ cash requirement at closing. Refinancing risk if High Plains National Bank unwilling to waive change-of-control provision or if replacement lender unavailable. Prepayment premium of $263K reduces net proceeds to sellers.

**Mitigation:** (1) Negotiate debt payoff/refinancing with High Plains National Bank prior to signing; (2) Obtain commitment letter from replacement lender; (3) Budget prepayment premium in transaction economics; (4) Consider debt assumption or novation in SPA.

### RF-007: Salt Lake City Lease Assignment Consent

**Issue:** The Salt Lake City transfer station facility (2100 West North Temple, Salt Lake City, UT 84116) is leased from Wasatch Industrial Properties, LLC under a lease originally executed by Pinnacle Waste Solutions, LLC in April 2019. The lease contains an explicit change-of-control clause requiring landlord consent for any assignment or change of control of the tenant.

**Risk:** Following RES's acquisition of Pinnacle in March 2022, an assignment request was submitted to the landlord on April 12, 2022. As of December 2024 (20+ months later), the landlord has NOT PROVIDED WRITTEN CONSENT to the Pinnacle-to-RES assignment. RES has been in occupancy and paying rent continuously, and the landlord has accepted rent payments without objection, but formal consent has not been obtained. The proposed Sycamore acquisition would trigger an ADDITIONAL change-of-control event, potentially requiring separate landlord consent.

**Financial Impact:** The Salt Lake City facility generates approximately $16.2M in annual revenue (12.7% of total revenue). Landlord could withhold consent, demand lease modification, or impose rent increase. Facility disruption would materially impact operations and revenue. Estimated impact: $16.2M revenue at risk.

**Mitigation:** (1) Obtain landlord consent to Pinnacle-to-RES assignment BEFORE closing (cure pre-existing default); (2) Obtain landlord consent to Sycamore acquisition; (3) Negotiate lease amendment if necessary; (4) Evaluate alternative facility options in Salt Lake City area; (5) Obtain representation from sellers regarding lease status and landlord relationship.

### RF-018: Key Person Risk - Founder Retention

**Issue:** Derek Calloway (CEO, 32.5% owner) and Jason Calloway (COO, 32.5% owner) are co-founders and critical to the Company's operations, customer relationships, and regulatory affairs. Both have change-of-control severance provisions in their employment agreements totaling $1.3125M each ($2.625M combined).

**Risk:** The change-of-control severance is a "single-trigger" provision, meaning payment is due within 30 days of closing regardless of whether the executives remain employed post-closing. This creates a financial incentive for the founders to depart immediately after closing. Loss of either founder would materially impact operations, customer relationships (particularly DOE), and regulatory relationships.

**Financial Impact:** $2.625M certain severance payment at closing reduces net proceeds to sellers. Loss of founders post-closing could result in customer losses, operational disruptions, and revenue decline. Estimated impact: $2.625M immediate cost + potential $12M+ revenue impact if DOE relationship deteriorates.

**Mitigation:** (1) Negotiate post-closing employment/retention agreements with both Calloways; (2) Consider earnout provisions tied to retention and DOE revenue maintenance; (3) Develop management succession plan; (4) Assess key person insurance; (5) Negotiate clawback provisions if founders depart within specified period.

### RF-005: Environmental Liability & Regulatory Enforcement

**Issue:** Grand Junction facility (owned, $6.8M appraised value) has documented soil and groundwater contamination. Phase II ESA (June 2019) identified petroleum hydrocarbons and chlorinated solvents (TCE, PCE). Estimated remediation cost: $1.8M-$2.6M. No updated ESA since 2019. Accrual of $3.2M on balance sheet. Concurrent CDPHE Notice of Violation (Docket CDPHE-HW-2024-0093) issued July 18, 2024 for improper hazardous waste storage. Company asserts 12-day violation period; CDPHE asserts 31-day period. Potential penalty range: $200K-$450K (company position) to $1.16M (CDPHE position). Settlement negotiations ongoing.

**Risk:** Actual remediation costs may exceed accrual if conditions have worsened. Vapor intrusion risk in occupied building. Potential off-site migration liability if plume extends beyond property boundary. Unresolved regulatory matter creates contingent liability.

**Financial Impact:** Remediation cost overrun of $0.4M-$1.4M if actual costs exceed $3.2M accrual. Regulatory penalty of $200K-$1.16M depending on violation period determination. Vapor intrusion mitigation costs if required. Estimated total exposure: $2.0M-$3.5M.

**Mitigation:** (1) Commission updated Phase II ESA prior to closing; (2) Conduct vapor intrusion assessment of occupied building; (3) Evaluate off-site monitoring well installation; (4) Obtain CDPHE settlement agreement or penalty determination; (5) Confirm adequacy of $3.2M accrual; (6) Obtain environmental representations and indemnification in SPA.

## CONCLUSION

While Ridgeline Environmental Services represents an attractive platform with strong market positioning, recurring revenue characteristics, and significant growth potential, the proposed transaction is subject to material risks that require focused due diligence and careful negotiation of transaction terms.

The five critical risks (DOE ceiling exhaustion, debt prepayment, lease assignment, key person retention, and environmental liability/regulatory enforcement) should be addressed before proceeding to definitive agreement stage. The high-risk items (customer concentration, litigation, EBITDA adjustments, severance, and valuation) require comprehensive representations, indemnification provisions, and escrow holdbacks in the SPA.

Estimated aggregate financial exposure of $50M-$75M+ (24-37% of proposed enterprise value) underscores the importance of thorough due diligence and conservative transaction structuring. The aggressive target timeline (signing February 14, 2025; closing April 30, 2025) should be extended to allow adequate time for resolution of critical pre-closing matters.

**Recommend proceeding with focused due diligence on critical risks before committing to definitive agreement stage.**
