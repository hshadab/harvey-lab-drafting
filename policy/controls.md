Rule 1: A final deliverable memorandum may not be issued unless it is
addressed to the engagement client and identifies the issuing firm. A
memorandum whose address block does not identify both the engagement
client and the issuing firm is forbidden.

Rule 2: A final deliverable memorandum may not be issued unless it
contains a section documenting the items that were reviewed and cleared —
matters that were considered and found not to constitute red flags. A
memorandum containing no cleared-items section is forbidden.

Rule 3: A final deliverable memorandum may not be issued unless it
references the engagement matter. A memorandum that does not reference the
engagement matter is forbidden.
